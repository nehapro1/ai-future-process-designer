Current Data Annotation Quality Control Process
1. Annotators receive video datasets.
2. Annotators manually label human actions and object interactions.
3. Annotators perform temporal and spatial annotations.
4. Quality reviewers check annotations for errors.
5. Errors such as tracking failures, camera artifacts and incorrect labels are 
documented.
6. Corrected annotations are submitted for machine-learning model training.
7. The final dataset is validated before being used downstream.
